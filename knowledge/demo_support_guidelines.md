# EduRadar Demonstration Support Guidance

These are fictional example guidelines for a hackathon. They are not policies issued by any school. Teachers must validate and replace them before actual use.

## Attendance check-ins
When attendance is below the configurable demo threshold of 75%, suggest a supportive private conversation to find out whether scheduling, transport, illness, or other circumstances may be affecting attendance. Do not assume lack of motivation or assign blame. A sustained decline can prompt a check-in even if overall attendance is higher.

## Assessment tutoring and isolated scores
Consider offering subject-specific tutoring when assessment scores are low over multiple observations. Avoid using one unusually poor assessment to conclude that a student's performance is declining. Review context and ask the teacher to verify evidence.

## Assignment completion
For repeated missed assignments, recommend discussing workload and offering planning or assignment support. If no assignments were due, completion percentage is not applicable, not zero.

## Engagement and mentoring
For sustained lower engagement, consider a supportive mentoring check-in. Engagement values are imperfect signals; they should not be treated as a diagnosis or measure of student worth.

## Follow-up practices
When a teacher chooses a support action, record the action, owner, date, and follow-up plan. At follow-up, record observed changes and comments. Observed improvement is not proof the action caused the change.

## Missing data and teacher review
Unknown attendance, assessment, assignment, or engagement values should remain missing, not zero. If there is insufficient evidence, suppress automatic priority and ask the teacher to review. Preserve original model scores separately from any teacher override and require a reason for overrides.
